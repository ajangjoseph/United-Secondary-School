About United Secondary School

Discover our identity, history, values and commitment to education.
United Secondary School was founded in 2020 and officially commenced its operations in 2022. Established by Ust. Maiwen Nyok Piol, the institution was founded with a vision of providing quality education and creating opportunities for learners to achieve academic excellence. The school provides education through three sections: Nursery, Primary and Secondary, offering learners an opportunity to grow academically from their early childhood years through secondary education.United Secondary School takes pride in its academic achievements, having won numerous trophies in different competitions, particularly debates. The school has also demonstrated outstanding academic performance, with students dominating the Top Ten positions in both the Secondary Senior Four Certificate examinations and the Primary Leaving Certificate examinations.With a team of national and international teachers, the institution continues to promote quality teaching, discipline, academic excellence and the development of future leaders.
